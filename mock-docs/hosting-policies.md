# Acme Hosting Policies

## Money Back Guarantee and Refunds
At Acme Hosting, we stand behind our services. We offer a strict 30-day money-back guarantee on all new shared hosting and VPS plans. If you cancel your account within the first 30 days of registration, you will receive a full refund. Please note that we do not offer refunds after the initial 30-day period. For example, if you request a refund after 45 days, it will be politely declined.

## Website Migrations
Moving to a new host shouldn't be a headache. Our expert migration team provides seamless transfers from your old host to Acme Hosting. We offer free migration assistance for all standard WordPress websites. Furthermore, if you are running an online store, we absolutely offer free migration for WooCommerce sites to ensure your business experiences zero downtime during the move.

## Server Uptime Guarantee
We guarantee a 99.9% network uptime. If your server experiences downtime exceeding our Service Level Agreement (SLA), you are eligible for service credits which can be applied to your next billing cycle.

## Technical Support Hours
Our technical support team is available 24/7/365 via live chat and our secure ticketing system. We aim to respond to all standard support tickets within 15 minutes. Please note that phone support is exclusively reserved for our Enterprise tier customers.
